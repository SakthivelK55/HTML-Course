LineShift

Place your 3 pieces first. After placement phase, move only one step (up, down, left, right). Make a full row or column to win.

Game Rules:

  • Green Player uses small pieces.
  • Red Player uses big pieces.
  • Each player has 3 pieces.
  • Players place pieces one by one.
  • After all 6 pieces are placed, movement phase starts.
  • You can move only ONE step: Up, Down, Left, Right.
  • Make 3 pieces in a row or column to win the game.
